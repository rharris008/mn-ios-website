When you work with M&N IOS, you get a single point of contact who coordinates everything — field activity, training schedules, retailer meetings, and reporting. You are not managing multiple suppliers or chasing different agencies for different outputs.

Case Studies

What we've delivered.

Brand Launch & Growth

From challenger brand to Australian market leader

Challenge

When Shark and Ninja first entered the Australian market, they faced strong competition from well-established household brands and needed a trusted retail execution partner to build their presence from the ground up.

Action

Mann & Noble secured premium shelf space at JB Hi-Fi, The Good Guys, Harvey Norman, and Bing Lee. We deployed a national in-store sales and training team, conducted live demonstrations, and negotiated exclusive promotional opportunities to accelerate brand penetration.

Outcome

SharkNinja transformed from a challenger brand into a market leader within 5 years, with Shark dominating Australian floorcare and Ninja ranked among the most sought-after names in kitchen appliances.

National Launch Execution

Dreame's flawless Q4 2024 national launch

Challenge

When Dreame launched nationally across major electrical retailers in late 2024, the brand needed flawless merchandising execution to stand out in a crowded floorcare category and ensure immediate sell-through momentum.

Action

M&N IOS implemented Dreame's POSM across Harvey Norman, The Good Guys, JB Hi-Fi, Bing Lee, and other key retailers. We ensured nationwide display compliance, monitored floor stock, and partnered with store teams to maintain premium merchandising standards throughout the launch.

Outcome

Dreame achieved nationwide merchandising compliance, strong visibility across all major retailers, and a significant uplift in sales during its critical Q4 2024 launch. Increased replenishment orders across retailers reflected strong customer uptake.

Market Entry Strategy

Toshiba rice cookers sold out in month one at Harvey Norman

Challenge

When Midea introduced its Toshiba rice cookers into Harvey Norman, the challenge was to prove sell-through success quickly and build retailer confidence for a broader SDA and MDA category expansion.

Action

M&N IOS sold in Toshiba rice cookers at store level, delivered in-store staff training and product education, and worked directly with store managers to build strong local buy-in and secure ranging across Harvey Norman stores.

Outcome

All stock sold within the first month (August 2024). Repeat orders secured at multiple stores, strengthening Harvey Norman's confidence. M&N IOS is now assisting Midea's MDA expansion with Bing Lee and Bi Rite, delivering staff training across new SKUs.
