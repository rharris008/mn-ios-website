How We Work

One team. One account manager.

When you work with M&N IOS, you get a single point of contact who coordinates everything — field activity, training schedules, retailer meetings, and reporting. You are not managing multiple suppliers or chasing different agencies for different outputs.

Case Studies

What we've delivered.

Brand and retailer details are anonymised. Results are based on M&N IOS-tracked data over the relevant project periods.

Visual Merchandising

Display compliance uplift for a floorcare brand

Challenge

A leading floorcare supplier was seeing display compliance below 65% across national accounts in QLD, SA and WA. Hero SKUs were floor-stacked rather than demonstrated on-display.

Action

M&N IOS deployed a dedicated national VM team with weekly compliance visits, same-day photo reporting and a 48-hour rectification protocol for off-display product.

Outcome

Display compliance lifted to 91% nationally within 10 weeks. Two stores previously without a live demonstration unit approved new gondola positions following rep engagement with store management.

+26% compliance
10 wks to target
2 new positions
In-Store Training

National training launch for a premium kitchen appliance

Challenge

A premium kitchen appliance brand launching into The Good Guys and Harvey Norman needed 1,200 floor staff trained across 180 stores within 6 weeks of ranging.

Action

M&N IOS developed a 45-minute in-store training module, deployed a national training team, and tracked completion store-by-store with a digital sign-off system.

Outcome

1,200 staff trained across 178 of 180 stores within the launch window. The brand's internal sell-through data showed 34% higher units per store in trained stores vs untrained stores at week 8.

1,200 staff trained
6 wks rollout
+34% sell-through
Head Office Account Management

Amazon Vendor Central turnaround for a consumer electronics brand

Challenge

A consumer electronics brand had Amazon Vendor Central active but unmanaged. Poor catalogue health, no A+ content, and recurring chargebacks were limiting visibility and margin.

Action

M&N IOS rebuilt the entire catalogue, resolved $52k in outstanding chargebacks, implemented A+ content across the core range, and negotiated two promotional deal submissions.

Outcome

Amazon revenue grew 2.8× over 12 months. Chargeback rate reduced from 21% to under 4%. Brand achieved top-3 category ranking in its primary segment.
